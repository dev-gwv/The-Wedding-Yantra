# Voice-over for the tutorial video: "My tasks"

This is the narration for the My tasks tutorial video (Team Task Management → My tasks), one
line per scene, in simple English. It follows a team member, Kabir: the tasks the owner gives
him, the ones he adds himself, and how he works through them. Make all 17 lines as **one file**
in ElevenLabs (see "Make it" below). The video is made so every scene lasts as long as its line,
so the voice and the screen always match.

## ElevenLabs settings

The same as the Facebook and Events videos, so they all sound like one person:

- **Voice:** "Nimmo – Clear Hinglish Customer Care", from the Voice Library.
- **Model:** Eleven Multilingual v2.
- **Settings:** speed 0.97 · stability 33% · similarity 66% · style 0 · speaker boost on.
- **Output:** MP3, 44.1 kHz.

## Make it

Copy this whole block into one ElevenLabs text box and download one MP3. It is cut into the 17
lines at the breaks, so keep exactly one break between lines and none inside a line.

```
Let's see how My tasks keeps all your work in one place, so nothing is missed. <break time="1.5s" />
Open My tasks from the menu. On a phone, tap More, then Tasks. <break time="1.5s" />
Every task for you is here, by date. Late tasks are at the top, in red. <break time="1.5s" />
Tap Doing, Stuck or To check to see just those. To check is your work, waiting to be checked. <break time="1.5s" />
To add your own task, tap Add task. Owners and managers see Give a task. <break time="1.5s" />
Write what needs doing, and pick when it's due: today, tomorrow or in a week. <break time="1.5s" />
Set the priority. Urgent and high tasks stand out on the list. <break time="1.5s" />
For a bigger job, tap More options, and add the steps, one per line. <break time="1.5s" />
Tap Add task. It's on your list right away. <break time="1.5s" />
Tap a task to open it. Tap Start when you begin, and tick each step as you go. <break time="1.5s" />
Waiting on someone? Tap I'm stuck, say what it's waiting on, and tap Mark as stuck. Whoever gave it will know. <break time="1.5s" />
Need more time? Tap Move date, pick a new day, say why, and tap Move it. <break time="1.5s" />
Some tasks are checked before they're done. Tap Hand in for a check, add a note, a link or a photo, and tap Hand in. <break time="1.5s" />
```
